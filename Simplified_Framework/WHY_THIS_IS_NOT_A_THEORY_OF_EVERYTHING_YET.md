# Why This Is Not a Theory of Everything Yet

- **Layer:** Simplified Framework
- **Status:** Plain-English project boundary
- **Last updated:** 3 October 2026

## Short answer

Because we have not solved it.

The project has a question, candidate mathematical machinery, a growing record of things that failed, increasingly difficult tests, and a method for trying to stop itself being fooled.

It does **not** yet have a demonstrated final theory of nature.

## What are we trying to do?

Quantum physics describes the small-scale world extremely well. General Relativity describes gravity and spacetime extremely well in its tested regime.

The project asks whether both could be effective descriptions of something deeper.

The working idea is not "quantum physics is wrong" or "Einstein was wrong." It is:

> **Could both be different large-scale behaviours produced by one deeper set of consistency rules?**

That hypothetical deeper rule layer is called **The Unbreakable Law**.

The research programme trying to find or falsify it is **The Unbreakable Method**.

## What would count as success?

A strong result would need to establish something like:

```text
minimal underlying rules
    ↓
quantum behaviour
```

and independently:

```text
physical response
    ↓
geometry
    ↓
spacetime
    ↓
gravity / GR
```

with the two branches eventually agreeing where they overlap.

That has not happened yet.

## What have we actually learned?

One major lesson is how easy it is to fool yourself.

Earlier models produced numbers that looked like three-dimensional geometry. Deliberately nasty control systems then produced similar numbers. Those tests were weakened or killed rather than protected.

That failure history is one of the main reasons the current Method exists.

## Where are we now?

### A possible microscopic quantum candidate

A candidate called HAM3 is being studied. It is genuinely quantum and genuinely interacting.

It has not been shown to produce our universe.

### A geometry-detection programme

Another part of the project is trying to determine whether an unknown physical response really behaves like smooth geometry without assuming "three dimensions" in advance.

That detector is not yet fully validated.

### A bridge problem

Even if the microscopic theory is good and the geometry detector is good, the physical response connecting them still has to be derived honestly.

### A gravity gap

Even genuine smooth geometry would not automatically mean General Relativity.

The remaining chain includes:

```text
geometry
    ↓
causal spacetime
    ↓
relativistic gravitational dynamics
    ↓
GR limit
    ↓
universal coupling to matter
```

### A quantum–gravity overlap problem

The final framework must explain how quantum behaviour and geometry coexist and interact, including difficult questions about entanglement, classicality and back-reaction.

## What should a reader believe?

At present:

- believe the mathematics where it is reproducible;
- treat a numerical result as an observation, not automatically as an interpretation;
- treat physical interpretations as provisional until independent tests support them;
- treat Parent candidates as candidates;
- treat open questions as open;
- attack everything.

## TL;DR

The Unbreakable Method is **not** claiming to have a Theory of Everything. It is trying to build a way of searching for a deeper framework without fooling itself. There are interesting candidate pieces, but major steps are still missing.
