# Naming and Scope

- **Project:** The Unbreakable Method
- **Scientific target:** The Unbreakable Law
- **Status:** Naming convention
- **Last updated:** 3 October 2026

The overall research programme is called **The Unbreakable Method**. It includes the models, tests, audits, failed paths and documentation used to investigate the underlying problem.

**The Unbreakable Law** is the name reserved for the hypothesised deeper rule structure being investigated: a minimal consistency structure capable of constraining admissible states, relations, compositions and effective behaviour strongly enough for familiar physics to emerge without being inserted by hand.

> **The Method is what we are doing. The Law is what we are testing whether nature has.**

The naming does not imply that such a law has been found.

## TL;DR

**The Unbreakable Method** is the research programme. **The Unbreakable Law** is the possible deeper rule structure the programme is testing for.
Unbreakable simply implies that the unknown can be explained through logic and math and information, it is not an assertation of accuracy.
