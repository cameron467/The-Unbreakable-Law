# The Unbreakable Method — Naming and Scope

> **Layer:** Project  
> **Status:** Project naming convention  
> **Last updated:** 3 October 2026  
> **Peer review:** Requested

## Naming convention

The overall research programme is called:

# The Unbreakable Method

The phrase **The Unbreakable Law** is reserved for a narrower scientific idea inside the project.

The distinction is:

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **The Unbreakable Method** | The research programme, repository, audit process, falsification workflow, documentation system, and method used to search for a deeper framework. |
| **The Unbreakable Law** | The hypothesised deeper rule or consistency structure that would determine which states, relations, compositions, transformations and emergent structures are physically admissible. |

The project name is therefore **not** a claim that a final law of nature has been discovered.

The project is a method for asking whether a sufficiently constrained underlying rule structure can be found, derived, falsified and independently audited.

---

## Why the distinction matters

Earlier versions of the project used **The Unbreakable Law** as the name of the whole programme.

That created an avoidable ambiguity:

> Was "The Unbreakable Law" the name of the research project, or a claim that the project had already found a final law?

The new naming removes that ambiguity.

The project is:

\[
\boxed{\text{The Unbreakable Method}}
\]

The hypothesised underlying rule layer is:

\[
\boxed{\text{The Unbreakable Law}}
\]

The Method may fail.

A candidate Law may fail.

Neither name is evidence that the scientific target has been reached.

---

## Working meaning of "The Unbreakable Law"

Within the framework, **The Unbreakable Law** does not mean "an equation that cannot be violated because we declared it fundamental."

It refers to the stronger target:

> a minimal consistency structure from which admissible carriers, states, relations, compositions and effective behaviour are constrained strongly enough that familiar physics can emerge without being inserted by hand.

A useful schematic is:

\[
\text{primitive consistency structure}
\rightarrow
\text{admissible states and relations}
\rightarrow
\text{composition and dynamics}
\rightarrow
\text{operational behaviour}
\rightarrow
\text{effective quantum / geometric physics}
\]

Whether such a structure exists is an open research question.

---

## What the Method is trying to prevent

The Unbreakable Method is designed to make it difficult to obtain a desired answer by accident.

It therefore emphasizes:

- independent construction of microscopic and geometric sides;
- frozen tests before confirmatory comparisons;
- explicit negative-result ledgers;
- false-positive controls;
- distinction between assumptions and derivations;
- hostile audit;
- reproduction before interpretation;
- clear separation between exploratory work and established claims;
- preservation of failed paths rather than rewriting the history after success.

The core methodological principle is:

> **Do not ask only whether a model can produce the desired answer. Ask whether it still produces the answer after every known way of cheating has been removed.**

## Epistemic labels used throughout the repository

Where relevant, technical pages should distinguish:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **ASSUMED / IMPORTED** | Supplied to the model rather than derived from it. |
| **DERIVED** | Follows from declared assumptions by mathematics or reproducible computation. |
| **OBSERVED** | A measured numerical, algebraic or experimental result. |
| **INTERPRETATION** | The physical meaning proposed for an observation or derivation. |
| **OPEN** | Required question not yet resolved. |
| **KILLED / RETIRED** | A candidate, diagnostic or interpretation that failed its stated test or was superseded by a stronger negative result. |

These labels are not confidence theatre. They exist to make it difficult for an interpretation to be silently promoted into a fact.

# TL;DR in common English

**The Unbreakable Method** is the project.

**The Unbreakable Law** is the possible deeper rule structure the project is searching for.

The Method is not proof that the Law exists, and the Law is not claimed to have been found. The naming is deliberately designed to keep the research programme separate from its hoped-for scientific conclusion.
