# From Geometry to General Relativity

- **Layer:** Academic Framework
- **Status:** Open closure map
- **Last updated:** 3 October 2026
- **Peer review:** Requested

A central risk in emergent-spacetime research is to identify a geometry-like signal and then silently treat it as gravity. This project does not adopt that shortcut.

> **Smooth geometry is not the same thing as Lorentzian spacetime, and Lorentzian spacetime is not the same thing as General Relativity.**

## Required closure chain

A full gravity result would require something like:

```text
physical response
    ↓
manifold-like geometry
    ↓
Lorentzian causal spacetime
    ↓
covariant gravitational dynamics
    ↓
GR in its tested low-energy regime
    ↓
universal matter coupling
```

Each arrow is a separate scientific obligation.

## 1. Physical response

**Question:** Does the microscopic Parent derive a physical operational response without inserting geometry by hand?

Relevant ingredients may include interventions, readouts, response functions, measures, composition rules, resource structure, causal information and cross-size comparison.

**Status:** **OPEN.** HAM3 has a genuine quantum response structure, but the full physical interface needed for a geometry claim has not yet been established.

## 2. Manifold-like geometry

**Question:** Does the response reconstruct one coherent geometry across scale?

The current programme tests combinations of:

- volume-growth dimension;
- differential/energy dimension;
- diffusion scaling;
- locality;
- anisotropy;
- compactification;
- Euclidean tangent behaviour;
- metric-measure convergence;
- Dirichlet-form and semigroup/resolvent convergence.

The decoder must not assume the answer is three-dimensional.

**Status:** **NOT ESTABLISHED.**

## 3. Lorentzian causal spacetime

A smooth spatial geometry is not yet relativistic spacetime. A viable emergent spacetime must recover a causal structure with the correct signature and transformation behaviour.

Relevant obligations include:

- a meaningful causal or null structure;
- a finite invariant causal speed where appropriate;
- no detectable preferred frame in the tested low-energy regime;
- consistent observer/frame transformations;
- propagation compatible with one effective causal geometry.

A finite propagation speed by itself is insufficient; many local systems have finite-speed propagation without Lorentz symmetry.

**Status:** **NOT ESTABLISHED.**

## 4. Covariant gravitational dynamics

Even a Lorentzian metric is not yet General Relativity. The geometry must evolve according to a suitable covariant dynamical structure.

The project has considered the Hypersurface Deformation Algebra as one possible consistency target because of its role in canonical GR. However:

> **HDA-like closure alone would not constitute a full derivation of GR.**

The relevant algebra, variables and interpretation would still need to arise independently rather than being inserted to resemble GR.

**Status:** **NOT ESTABLISHED.**

## 5. GR as an effective limit

A deeper theory need not make classical GR fundamental. It must reproduce GR in the regime where GR is known to work.

Future obligations include testing appropriate limits of:

- weak-field gravitational response;
- Newtonian behaviour;
- relativistic corrections;
- gravitational-wave propagation;
- curvature response;
- conservation structure;
- precision low-energy behaviour.

**Status:** **NOT ESTABLISHED.**

## 6. Universal matter coupling

A geometry can exist without being the geometry seen universally by matter. A gravity claim therefore requires testing whether distinct probe sectors reconstruct and respond to the same effective geometry.

Questions include:

- Do different probe sectors share the same causal structure?
- Does free-fall behaviour become universal in the relevant regime?
- Is the effective metric probe-independent?
- Are apparent equivalence-principle violations absent or suppressed where experiments require?

**Status:** **NOT ESTABLISHED.**

## 7. Quantum–gravity overlap

The project ultimately aims at a common deeper structure, not merely a classical gravity simulator. A successful framework should therefore permit regimes in which:

- quantum observables remain meaningful;
- entanglement and noncommutativity remain possible;
- an effective geometry is simultaneously reconstructable;
- the quantum and geometric descriptions couple consistently.

The working possibility is an overlap region rather than a sharp boundary:

```text
microscopic quantum
    ↓
quantum + proto-geometric
    ↓
quantum fields on effective geometry
    ↓
increasingly classical geometry
```

**Status:** **MATHEMATICALLY PERMITTED; NOT DEMONSTRATED.**

## Required impostors

A gravity decoder should reject or correctly classify systems that mimic isolated gravitational features. Relevant controls include:

- finite-speed systems with a preferred frame;
- smooth metrics with non-Einstein dynamics;
- fractal or Carnot geometries;
- compactified spaces;
- strongly anisotropic spaces;
- graph systems with artificial causal cones;
- modified-gravity lookalikes;
- response interfaces that already encode the target geometry.

## Interpretation rule

A claim should stop at the highest stage actually established.

For example:

> "The response is consistent with a smooth three-dimensional effective geometry"

does **not** imply:

> "The model has derived spacetime."

Likewise, reconstructing Lorentzian causal structure would not by itself establish Einstein dynamics.

## TL;DR

The current geometry programme asks whether a response behaves like real smooth geometry. That is important, but it is not yet gravity. A full result still needs geometry → causal spacetime → gravitational dynamics → GR limit → universal matter coupling.
