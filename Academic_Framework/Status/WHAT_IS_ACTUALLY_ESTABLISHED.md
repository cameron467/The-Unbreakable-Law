## What is established inside the project

"Established inside the project" means mathematically reproduced, explicitly derived from declared inputs, or preserved as a documented negative result. It does not mean established as a new law of nature.

### Several earlier geometry diagnostics were too weak

The project has already rejected or demoted multiple attractive diagnostics, including:

- scalar or spectral dimension as a sufficient geometry certificate;
- the largest-relative-gap decoder;
- a preregistered ordered-eigenvalue/Weyl gate that failed clean calibration;
- standalone three-point Gaussian closure;
- local distance/MDS tangent tests used as standalone evidence;
- response-metric persistence used as a sufficient certificate.

This matters because favourable outputs were not protected after stronger controls showed they could be misleading.

### Parent #1 is retired

Earlier graph-based Parent #1 results produced geometry-like signals, but hostile controls showed that compact, twin, product and related adversarial structures could reproduce important diagnostics.

**Current claim:** Parent #1 does not establish autonomous emergent 3D geometry.

The failure remains part of the public audit record.

### The geometry side is a decoder architecture, not a discovery claim

The current programme asks whether a frozen operational response reconstructs one coherent geometry across scale. Live targets include:

- dimension without presetting dimension 3;
- locality and persistence;
- anisotropy and compactification;
- local differential/energy rank;
- Euclidean tangent behaviour;
- metric-measure convergence;
- Dirichlet-form, semigroup or resolvent convergence.

No serious Parent #2 has yet passed a frozen confirmatory pipeline.

### HAM3 is genuinely quantum but remains a candidate

HAM3 is a gauged two-matrix quantum mechanical system with a Hilbert space, density operators, noncommuting observables, unitary dynamics, gauge-invariant measurements and an interacting commutator-squared term.

That establishes that HAM3 is not merely a classical graph optimiser. It does **not** establish emergent locality, geometry, a preferred subsystem decomposition, Bell correlations between derived spatial subsystems, General Relativity or a continuum physical world.

### A standard route exists from suitable operator algebras to subsystem structure

Finite-dimensional operator algebra gives an exact conditional statement:

$$
\text{factor algebra} + \text{commutant}
\Longrightarrow
\text{tensor-product subsystem structure}.
$$

This is relevant because a model need not begin by declaring spatially labelled subsystems. The project has **not** yet shown that HAM3 naturally generates the required distinguished factor algebras.

## What is imported rather than explained

Depending on the candidate and lane, current imported structure includes items such as:

- ordinary quantum canonical mechanics;
- Born probabilities;
- Hilbert-space structure;
- some laboratory controllability assumptions;
- the chosen carrier algebra;
- the finite-rank family label \(N\);
- some resource/cost conventions;
- some cross-system composition rules.

Imported structure is not automatically a flaw. It becomes a problem only when an imported ingredient is later described as though the model derived it.

## Claim discipline

Preferred wording:

- "The candidate permits..." rather than "Nature uses..."
- "The response is consistent with..." rather than "The response proves..."
- "A subsystem decomposition can be reconstructed if..." rather than "The Parent has produced particles..."
- "This diagnostic survives the current controls..." rather than "This diagnostic identifies spacetime..."

## What would materially change the status

A major scientific promotion would require, at minimum, a combination of:

1. a serious Parent #2 frozen independently of downstream geometry;
2. a derived operational interface rather than a hand-selected one;
3. a geometry decoder frozen before seeing the Parent response;
4. held-out positive and negative controls;
5. a successful blind comparison;
6. hostile independent reproduction;
7. continuum and scaling evidence;
8. a validated route from geometry to Lorentzian spacetime and gravitational dynamics;
9. correct coupling to the quantum sector;
10. explicit accounting of assumptions, imports and surviving alternatives.

## TL;DR

The project has built increasingly hard tests and has killed several of its own earlier favourite ideas. It has **not** yet derived spacetime, General Relativity, entanglement from a deeper law, or a final Parent theory. HAM3 and the current geometry decoder are research machinery, not discoveries of nature.
